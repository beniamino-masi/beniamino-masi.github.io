About Me

I am a Post-doctoral Researcher at the University of Pisa (since December 2025), working on the research project WATERSENS. I am a comparative political scientist: my work uses cross-national comparison and policy analysis to study how political organizations and institutions adapt to structural change — a single thread running from the long-run transformation of political parties in Western Europe to the governance of the ecological and water transition in the European Union.

Previously, I served as a Post-doctoral Researcher at the University of Padua (2023–2025), working on the project "Governing the 'Just Transition': Eco-social Politics and Policies in the EU".

I received my PhD in Political Science from the University of Pisa in 2024, with a thesis titled "Patterns of party organizational change. Convergence and disintermediation in Europe (1969-2017)".

My research interests span the evolution of political parties in Western Europe, intra-party democracy, and the politics of the green and just transition. Methodologically I work in the comparative tradition, combining cross-national designs with case-based process-tracing and mixed methods. I also serve as the Communication Officer for the "Political Parties, Party Systems and Elections" Research Network of the Council for European Studies.
